Case files

🪙 The C$20 million gold heist (Toronto Pearson, 2023)

  • On April 17, 2023, a shipment of 6,600 gold bars (worth about $20 million) and about $2.5 million in foreign currency arrived at Toronto Pearson from Zurich. Police say a suspect showed a fraudulent air waybill and drove off with the shipment. (CP24, 2025)
  • Brink’s sued Air Canada. In January 2025 the Federal Court limited the airline’s liability to 9,988 SDR, just over C$18,000, because Brink’s had not made a special declaration of interest in delivery. In practice that means declaring a higher value in advance and paying a surcharge. (CP24, 2025; de Vries, 2026)
  • The 9,988 SDR is the old air limit of 22 SDR per kg applied to about 454 kg of gold and notes. The limit rose to 26 SDR per kg on December 28, 2024. (de Vries, 2026; ICAO, 2024)
  • Air Canada confirmed on August 28, 2026 that it had settled with Brink’s. Neither side has said what was paid. (de Vries, 2026)

Lesson: “the carrier pays while it holds my cargo” is a common misconception, because most carriers’ liability is limited (Seneca Polytechnic, 2026). The legal limit is not insurance. A declared value (for a fee) or a cargo policy is what protects the value of the goods.

The replay uses today’s limit of 26 SDR per kg, so the carrier’s share comes out slightly higher than the court’s. Its Incoterm (FCA, you as the buyer) is my own assumption.

🧭 The forwarder who never insured

  • A freight forwarder reported never buying cargo insurance in five years, with mostly FOB or EXW shipments from China to other Asian countries.
  • Why that can make sense: under EXW and FOB the buyer carries the risk once the goods are collected or loaded, so insuring is the buyer’s call. Buyers often hold their own policy. Routes are short. Most shipments arrive fine.
  • The gap: “most arrive fine” is not a guarantee. On an ocean leg, general average can bill an owner whose goods are untouched, and a deposit is due before release. If nobody has cover, a total loss lands on whoever held the risk.

Not a criticism. It is a risk decision, made visible. The cargo in the demo is my own example, and the odds are illustrative.
